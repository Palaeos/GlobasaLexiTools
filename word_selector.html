3b. Contrasts other languages make

English translation senses can lump meanings that other languages keep apart (happy: lasting 幸福 vs momentary 嬉しい). Each source word is tried as a pivot; a contrast is kept when languages of at least two families separate it, or mark it with a word of their own for one side.
